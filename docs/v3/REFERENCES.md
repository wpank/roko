## 00 -- Lifecycle and Finite Agency

**[Ray, 1991]** *An Approach to the Synthesis of Life.* Artificial Life II, Addison-Wesley, 1992.
Brief annotation: Tierra showed digital evolution halts without a reaper; resource constraints drive innovation. Grounds knowledge decay and budget-driven urgency.

**[Lenski et al., 2003]** *The Evolutionary Origin of Complex Features.* Nature, 423, 139--144.
Brief annotation: Complex features in Avida require generational turnover. Grounds Delta-frequency knowledge refresh cycles.

**[Vostinar et al., 2019]** *Suicidal Selection: Programmed Cell Death Evolves as Adaptive Behavior under Spatial Structure.* Evolution, 73(5).
Brief annotation: Programmed cell death evolves adaptively under spatial structure. Informs resource-bounded agent lifecycle.

**[Werfel et al., 2017]** *How Short-Lived Agents Can Collectively Build Long-Lived Structures.* Artificial Life, 23(3).
Brief annotation: Short-lived agents produce durable artifacts. Grounds the separation between ephemeral agent sessions and persistent knowledge.

**[Dohare et al., 2024]** *Loss of Plasticity in Deep Continual Learning.* Nature, 632.
Brief annotation: Neural networks progressively lose the ability to learn. Grounds the requirement for periodic knowledge refresh.

**[Vela et al., 2022]** *Temporal Quality Degradation in AI Models.* Journal of Data and Information Quality, 14(1).
Brief annotation: AI model quality degrades over time without maintenance. Motivates knowledge freshness tracking via half-lives.

**[Sculley et al., 2015]** *Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems.* NeurIPS.
Brief annotation: ML systems accumulate hidden technical debt. Grounds the resource lifecycle and cleanup mechanisms.

**[Arbesman, 2012]** *The Half-Life of Facts: Why Everything We Know Has an Expiration Date.* Current/Penguin.
Brief annotation: Knowledge has measurable half-lives. Grounds Ebbinghaus-style decay parameters in the knowledge store.

**[Ord, 2025]** *Is there a half-life for the success rates of AI agents?* Working paper.
Brief annotation: Empirical finding that agent success degrades with horizon length. Motivates bounded task durations.

**[Sims, 2003]** *Implications of Rational Inattention.* Journal of Monetary Economics, 50(3), 665--690.
Brief annotation: Rational finite-capacity agents optimally ignore some information. Grounds the VCG attention auction and T0 suppression.

**[Orseau & Ring, 2011]** *Self-Modification and Mortality in Artificial Agents.* AGI 2011.

**[Orseau & Armstrong, 2016]** *Safely Interruptible Agents.* UAI 2016.

**[Kreps et al., 1982]** *Rational Cooperation in the Finitely Repeated Prisoners' Dilemma.* Journal of Economic Theory, 27(2), 245--252.
Brief annotation: Rational cooperation emerges under finite horizons. Informs agent cooperation strategies.

**[Ohtsuki et al., 2006]** *A Simple Rule for the Evolution of Cooperation on Graphs and Social Networks.* Nature, 441, 502--505.
Brief annotation: Cooperation evolves on graphs when benefit/cost exceeds average degree. Grounds cooperative dynamics in the Agent Mesh.

**[Nakamaru et al., 1997]** *The Evolution of Cooperation in a Lattice-Structured Population.* Journal of Theoretical Biology.

**[Smith, 1992]** *Byte-sized Evolution.* Nature, 355, 772--773.
Brief annotation: Commentary on digital evolution. Contextualizes the evolutionary framing.

**[Shuvaev et al., 2024]** *Encoding Innate Ability Through a Genomic Bottleneck.* PNAS, 121(39).
Brief annotation: Compression through a genomic bottleneck forces generalization. Grounds knowledge backup compression.

**[Hinton, 2022]** *The Forward-Forward Algorithm: Some Preliminary Investigations.* Working paper.
Brief annotation: Alternative learning algorithm without backpropagation. Informs local learning rule research.

**[Ororbia & Friston, 2023]** *Mortal Computation.* Working paper.
Brief annotation: Computation as inherently bounded by physical substrate. Theoretical context for resource-bounded cognition.

**[Baldwin, 1896]** *A New Factor in Evolution.* American Naturalist, 30, 441--451.
