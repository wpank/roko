# 39-05 Biological Analogues -- Annotated Reference Map

> Biological systems providing structural analogies for Roko's cognitive architecture:
> foraging, immune selection, niche construction, morphogenetic specialization.

> **v3 depth file** -- updated 2026-09-15.

## Optimal Foraging Theory

**[Charnov, 1976]** *Optimal Foraging, the Marginal Value Theorem.* Theoretical Population Biology, 9(2), 129--136.
When to leave a depleting resource patch. Agents switch tasks when marginal value falls below the average rate of return. Grounds knowledge foraging in Router.

**[Stephens & Krebs, 1986]** *Foraging Theory.* Princeton University Press.
Comprehensive framework for optimal information gathering under cost constraints. Grounds predictive foraging.

**[Pirolli & Card, 1999]** *Information Foraging.* Psychological Review, 106(4), 643--675.
"Information scent" maps to HDC similarity scores guiding knowledge retrieval.

**[Lacosse et al., 2026]** *LLMs Exhibit Same Foraging Patterns as Humans.* arXiv:2603.01822.
LLMs exhibit the same information foraging patterns as humans. Validates the MVT-based approach.

**[Anonymous, 2025]** *Foraging in Modern Semantic Spaces.* arXiv:2511.12759.
Information foraging theory applied to modern LLM semantic spaces.

---

## Immune System Analogues

**[de Castro & Timmis, 2002]** *Artificial Immune Systems.* Springer.
AIS computational framework. Grounds the immune Graph's danger detection and self-tolerance.

**[Ramsdell & Fowlkes, 1990]** *Clonal Deletion versus Clonal Anergy.* Science, 248(4961).
95--98% thymocyte death during development. Massive filtering produces collectively intelligent systems. Validates aggressive gate filtering.

---

## Niche Construction

**[Odling-Smee, Laland & Feldman, 2003]** *Niche Construction.* Princeton University Press.
Organisms modify their environment, modifying selection pressures. Agents modify their Substrate (knowledge store), modifying context for future runs.

---

## Self-Organization and Pattern Formation

**[Kauffman, 1993]** *The Origins of Order.* Oxford University Press.
Order emerges at the "edge of chaos." Roko's adaptive clock targets this regime. Grounds autocatalytic metrics.

**[Turing, 1952]** *The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis.* Phil. Trans. Royal Society B, 237(641), 37--72.
Reaction-diffusion pattern formation. Pheromone emission (activation) and decay (inhibition) create emergent specialization.

**[Gierer & Meinhardt, 1972]** *A Theory of Biological Pattern Formation.* Kybernetik, 12(1), 30--39.
Formal activator-inhibitor model. Short-range activation (local reinforcement) and long-range inhibition (global decay).

**[Kondo & Miura, 2010]** *Reaction-Diffusion Model as a Framework.* Science, 329(5999), 1616--1620.
Modern confirmation of reaction-diffusion models. Validates the Turing-pattern analogy for agent specialization.

---

## Stress Response and Optimal Arousal

**[Yerkes & Dodson, 1908]** *The Relation of Strength of Stimulus to Rapidity of Habit-Formation.* Journal of Comparative Neurology and Psychology, 18(5), 459--482.
Inverted-U arousal-performance curve. Grounds the Daimon's arousal modulation of tier routing.

---

## Superorganism and Collective Biology

**[Holldobler & Wilson, 2008]** *The Superorganism.* W.W. Norton.
Insect colonies as superorganisms. When C-Factor > 1.0, the Collective is a superorganism.

**[Camazine et al., 2001]** *Self-Organization in Biological Systems.* Princeton University Press.
Agent coordination emerges from simple local rules without central control.

---

## Morphogenetic Specialization

**[Murray, 2003]** *Mathematical Biology II.* Springer.
Mathematical framework for spatial biological processes. Foundation for agent specialization in mesh topology.

**[Lotka, 1925]** *Elements of Physical Biology.* Williams & Wilkins.
Population dynamics. Grounds scaling analysis for agent populations.

**[Volterra, 1926]** *Fluctuations in the Abundance of a Species.* Nature, 118, 558--560.
Lotka-Volterra equations: competing strategies oscillate between exploration and exploitation.

---

## Quorum Sensing

**[Nealson, Platt & Hastings, 1970]** *Cellular Control of the Bacterial Luminescent System.* Journal of Bacteriology, 104(1), 313--322.
First description of quorum sensing. When enough agents produce signals above threshold, collective actions trigger.

**[Miller & Bassler, 2001]** *Quorum Sensing in Bacteria.* Annual Review of Microbiology, 55, 165--199.
Universal quorum sensing. Validates quorum-based triggering in Collectives.

Stigmergy outperforms Levy flight in hazardous environments. Validates stigmergic coordination for uncertain domains.
