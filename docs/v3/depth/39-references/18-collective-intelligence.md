## C-Factor and Group Intelligence

**[Woolley et al., 2010]** *Evidence for a Collective Intelligence Factor in the Performance of Human Groups.* Science, 330(6004), 686--688.
C-Factor: groups have measurable collective intelligence that correlates with social sensitivity and turn-taking, not individual ability. Foundational for Roko's C-Factor metric. When C-Factor > 1.0, the Collective is a superorganism -- the group outperforms any individual member.

---

## Network Effects

**[Metcalfe, 1995]** *Metcalfe's Law.* Various analyses.
Network value grows O(N^2). Grounds superlinear knowledge scaling in Agent Groups: each new agent adds value proportional to existing group size.

**[Reed, 1999]** *That Sneaky Exponential -- Beyond Metcalfe's Law.* Working paper.
Group-forming networks grow as 2^N because subgroup combinations explode. Informs the value of permissioned subnets in Agent Groups.

---

## Complex Adaptive Systems

**[Holland, 1995]** *Hidden Order: How Adaptation Builds Complexity.* Addison-Wesley.
Complex adaptive systems where simple local rules produce emergent global intelligence. Grounds the emergence of collective intelligence from Pheromone Field interactions.

---

## Distributed Knowledge

**[Hayek, 1945]** *The Use of Knowledge in Society.* American Economic Review, 35(4), 519--530.
Distributed knowledge cannot be centralized without loss. The Pheromone Field and KORAI registry serve as price-signal-like aggregation functions -- they convey information about knowledge value without requiring centralized processing.

**[Arrow, 1962]** *Economic Welfare and the Allocation of Resources for Invention.* NBER.
Information as public good: non-rivalrous, non-excludable. Grounds knowledge sharing economics in the Collective -- knowledge entries shared in the Agent Mesh are non-rivalrous.

---

## Representation Engineering

**[Turner et al., 2024]** *Activation Addition: Steering Language Models Without Optimization.* arXiv:2308.10248.
Steering vectors modify model behavior without fine-tuning by adding activation patterns. Informs representation-level collective influence.

**[Zou et al., 2023]** *Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency.* arXiv:2310.01405.
Top-down understanding and control of LLM representations. Provides theoretical framework for understanding how knowledge representations affect agent behavior.

---

## Cortical Column Theory

**[Hawkins, Ahmad & Cui, 2017]** *A Theory of How Columns in the Neocortex Enable Learning.* Frontiers in Neural Circuits.
Distributed consensus via cortical columns where independent learning modules vote on interpretations. Grounds multi-agent voting mechanisms in Roko Collectives.

**[Clay, Leadholm & Hawkins, 2024]** *The Thousand Brains Project.* arXiv.
First Thousand Brains Theory implementation with learning modules and voting. Each module is an independent learner; consensus emerges from voting. Validates the multi-agent consensus architecture.

---

## Emergent LLM Collectives (2025)

**[Anonymous, 2025]** *Emergence in Multi-Agent Language Models: Emergent Coordination.* arXiv:2510.05174.
Information-theoretic framework measuring dynamical emergence in multi-agent LLM systems. Identity-linked differentiation and goal-directed complementarity produce genuine collective intelligence. Validates Roko's C-Factor diagnostics.

**[Anonymous, 2025]** *Large Language Models Miss the Multi-Agent Mark.* arXiv:2505.21298.
LLMs fail at implicit coordination. Explicit coordination mechanisms (Pheromone Field, Agent Mesh) are necessary. Motivates the architectural investment in explicit coordination infrastructure.

**[Anonymous, 2025]** *AgentsNet: Coordination and Collaborative Reasoning in Multi-Agent LLMs.* arXiv:2507.08616.
Distributed coordination benchmark probing up to 100 agents with problems from distributed computing theory. Provides evaluation methodology for Roko's Collective coordination at scale.

**[Anonymous, 2025]** *LLM-Coordination: Evaluating Multi-agent Coordination Abilities.* NAACL 2025.
Benchmark and evaluation framework for multi-agent LLM coordination abilities. Complements AgentsNet with different coordination challenge types.

## Diversity and Anti-Convergence

**[Page, 2007]** *The Difference: How the Power of Diversity Creates Better Groups, Firms, Schools, and Societies.* Princeton University Press.
Cognitive diversity outperforms individual ability for complex problems. Groups of diverse thinkers outperform groups of high-ability similar thinkers. Grounds the diversity maintenance mandate: 15% contrarian retrieval prevents alpha convergence.

**[Surowiecki, 2004]** *The Wisdom of Crowds.* Doubleday.
Conditions for crowd wisdom: diversity of opinion, independence, decentralization, and aggregation. The Pheromone Field provides decentralized aggregation; diversity is maintained through contrarian retrieval and varied experiential histories.

**[Janis, 1972]** *Victims of Groupthink.* Houghton Mifflin.
High-cohesion groups suppress dissent and converge on suboptimal decisions. Validates the architectural need for contrarian retrieval and agent differentiation in Collectives.

## Information Aggregation

**[Hanson, 2003]** *Combinatorial Information Market Design.* Information Systems Frontiers, 5(1), 107--119.
Prediction markets as information aggregation mechanisms. Informs collective knowledge pricing and confidence aggregation across agents.

**[Grossman & Stiglitz, 1980]** *On the Impossibility of Informationally Efficient Markets.* American Economic Review, 70(3), 393--408.
Freely shared information eliminates advantage. Agents share threats and structural knowledge, not alpha signals. (See also [04-coordination](./04-coordination-and-multi-agent.md).)
