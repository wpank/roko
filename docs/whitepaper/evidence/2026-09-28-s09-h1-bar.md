# S09: Experiments, hypotheses and budget (pre-registration)
- v1.1 (2026-09-28, w0-recon-B1): D1–D3 applied: P1 = three arms plus a 48-task frontier × Roko probe; `fd_claude` = `claude-opus-5-5`, `fd_claude_lite` = `claude-sonnet-5`, `fd_codex` = gpt-5.5; H1 bar X = 0.90, Y = 0.30 decided [R.S09-15]
- **Design (D1).** Three arms, `cheap_direct`, `roko_full` and `fd_claude`, carry H1 and H2; `fr_claude` is a
  48-task probe that serves H3 and the frontier harness contrast. `fd_claude_lite`, `fd_codex` and `fd_api`
  are secondary frontier-direct comparators, and `hybrid` is a replay. The paper and S10 describe P1 as three
  arms plus a probe, never as a full 2×2.
- **Envelope procedure (H1 primary).** For j = 1…5, on tasks with ℓ ≤ j, compute
  R_j = VS rate(`roko_full`) / VS rate(`fd_claude`) and C_j = $/VS(`roko_full`) / $/VS(`fd_claude`).
  Claim level j if the 95% bootstrap lower bound of R_j ≥ X = 0.90 **and** the upper bound of C_j ≤ Y = 0.30 (X and Y decided by the author, D3).
  Test in order and stop at the first failure; E* is the last level claimed (0 if ℓ1 fails). The bounds are taken
  at H1's Holm local level a ((1 − a) two-sided bounds, e.g. a = 0.05/7 if H1 is tested first; §4.1). Within
  H1 the fixed sequence needs no further adjustment.
**H1: dependability economics (the envelope).**
